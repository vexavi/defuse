HOW TO DEFUSE
1Ask for the two numbers.
2Ask for the light color.
3Use the table above to find the math symbol.
4Solve the calculation and tell the Defuser the answer.
5Get 2 correct calculations to clear the module.
For addition and subtraction, both displayed numbers are two digits. For multiplication and division, the numbers may be one or two digits. The number-pad order is randomized at the start of each bomb and stays the same during that bomb. After every correct or wrong answer, the numbers and light change.
