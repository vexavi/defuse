LOW-LEVEL ESL COMMUNICATION GAME

ESL Defuse

One student has the bomb. One student has the manual. Talk in English and defuse it before time runs out.

💣 Defuser

See and control the bomb. Describe what you see to your partner.

📘 Manual Reader

Do not look at the bomb. Use the manual and tell the Defuser what to do.

Classroom setup: Put the students back-to-back, or place a folder between the two screens so the Manual Reader cannot see the bomb.

MODULE 2

Wires

Ask: “How many wires?” and “What colors?”

  1. 4 wires: If there is a RED wire, cut the first RED wire. If there is no red, cut the last wire.
  2. 5 wires: If there are two or more YELLOW wires, cut the second YELLOW wire. Otherwise, cut the first BLUE wire.
  3. 6 wires: If there is a BLACK wire, cut the first BLACK wire. Otherwise, cut the third wire.
